\section{Observed Output Across Machines}

Requirement~(c) asks what was observed on each machine involved. Because the toolkit is a
scanner whose primary evidence \emph{is} its printed output, the console logs below are the
core artefacts; the placeholders mark where the corresponding demonstration screenshots
are inserted.

\subsection{Attacker PC}
The attacker machine shows the full chain: scan states, the fingerprint verdict with its
evidence, and the exploit results (all illustrated in Section~\ref{sec:steps}). In the
active-defense run, the attacker also observes the chain being severed mid-execution —
the first exploit completes, then subsequent connections time out with
\texttt{WinError~10060} as the victim's firewall drops the attacker's packets.

\subsection{Victim PC}
The victim side shows the other half of each exploit. The vulnerable FTP and HTTP servers
log the anonymous login, the web-shell upload and its CGI execution, and the traversal
request. In SSH loud mode the victim's desktop receives the trophy artefacts and a spoken
alert.

\begin{logbox}
[victim FTP]  230 anonymous login  |  STOR uploads/shell.py  |  DELE uploads/shell.py
[victim HTTP] GET /uploads/shell.py?cmd=whoami  ->  200 (executed)
[victim HTTP] GET /..%5c..%5cwindows%5cwin.ini  ->  200 (file served)
\end{logbox}
